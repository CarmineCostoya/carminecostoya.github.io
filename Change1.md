# Change 1: Strengthen the Professional Story

## Goal

Make the portfolio more useful to recruiters by clarifying Carmine's cross-functional positioning and surfacing a small set of verified, measurable outcomes.

## Planned changes

- Add a Selected Impact section to the homepage.
- Use only metrics and claims contained in the supplied resume.
- Strengthen the About page's explanation of how design, technology, and business connect in Carmine's work.
- Add concise, outcome-oriented bullets to the relevant experience entries.

## Expected result

A recruiter should be able to understand Carmine's professional focus and see evidence of business, product, and team impact in under a minute.
